Where to start with budgeting

If you only read one thing here, read the paycheck split: four walls first, then an automatic transfer, then permission money. It is the order that makes everything else possible, and the whole setup takes about twenty minutes.

Most budgeting advice assumes a stable income and a spreadsheet habit. Yours probably has neither yet. So these posts are built around decisions you make once: what counts as a wall, which account the savings live in, and what happens in a month that goes wrong. Percentages come second.

Two rules that survive a bad month

  • Automate the saving the day after payday, so it stops being a monthly decision.
  • When the spending account hits zero, wait. Never borrow from the walls.
