The mistakes worth reading about before you make them

Most of these cost money quietly. A subscription that renews for a year. A card balance that only ever gets the minimum. A buy-now-pay-later plan that turns one $90 purchase into four separate decisions. None of them feel like a mistake at the time, which is exactly why they live on this shelf.

Every post here names the cost over twelve months rather than the sticker price, then gives you the fix in the order that protects your savings and your credit score first. Where there is a version that costs nothing, that version comes first.

If you only fix one thing

Cover the minimums, then make the savings automatic, then worry about the rest. Getting that order wrong is the single most expensive habit in your early twenties, and it is fixable in about ten minutes inside your banking app.
